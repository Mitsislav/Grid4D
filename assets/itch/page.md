## Description

> **Grid4D** is a free, open-source 3D content creation suite for Windows, macOS
> and Linux. It is built around a calm, familiar layout — one viewport, an Object
> Manager, an Attribute Manager and a timeline — so you spend your time making
> things instead of hunting for buttons.
>
> **Model** — parametric primitives, polygon modelling (extrude, inset, bevel,
> bridge, loop and line cuts, weld), Subdivision Surfaces, splines drawn with the
> Pen, and surfaces made from them: Extrude, Lathe, Sweep, Loft and 3D text.
>
> **Deform and clone** — Bend, Twist, Taper, Bulge and FFD deformers; a Cloner
> (linear, radial, grid, on a surface) with Plain and Random effectors, all live
> and non-destructive.
>
> **Animate** — keyframes on positions and on object parameters, Autokeying, an
> F-curve editor, and rigid-body dynamics on objects and on clones.
>
> **Materials and UVs** — pictures on every channel of a material
> (colour, roughness, metallic, normal and bump, emission, cut-outs, glass,
> clear coat), layered Material tags with projections, and a UV Editor with
> unwrapping, relaxing and packing.
>
> **Render** (new in 1.2) — a built-in path tracer with area lights, a
> daylight sky and HDR surroundings, light linking, depth of field and motion
> blur, subsurface scattering and displacement; adaptive sampling and a
> denoiser for clean pictures sooner; a Render View that keeps rendering while
> you work, a render region, a Render Queue, and render passes in one
> multi-layer OpenEXR. In the viewport: textures, the sky, shadows and ambient
> occlusion, up to four views.
>
> **Work your way** — dockable layouts, Layers and Takes, Dark / Darker / Light
> themes, English or Greek, glTF 2.0 and OBJ import and export with materials and
> pictures, Rhai scripts with
> a Script Manager, plugins written in Rust, autosave and crash recovery.
>
> Grid4D is free and stays free: the full source is on GitHub under MIT OR
> Apache-2.0. Paying here is optional — it simply supports development. Thank
> you!
>
> **Requirements:** a graphics card with Vulkan, Direct3D 12 or Metal; 64-bit
> Windows 10+, macOS 11+ or a recent Linux.
>
> **First start:** the downloads are not code-signed yet. On Windows choose
> *More info ▸ Run anyway*; on macOS right-click the app and choose *Open*.
